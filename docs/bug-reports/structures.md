## Scenarios covered

Array [A,B,C] inserts X at 1 and removes B. Capacity-1 insertion fails without mutation, grow then retry succeeds. Linked a/b/x preserves references on insert/remove; b.next=a is a bounded CYCLE until repaired. Queue duplicate A arrivals preserve identity and FIFO. Invalid fields, references, infinity and fabricated event histories fail validation.
