# src/engine/modules/fourModule.test.ts

## Scenarios covered

Twelve Array/Linked List/Queue teaching activities: wrong rule, wrong or empty fresh evidence, reset in transfer, correct independent transfer, cross-task identity and replay. Four-module storage: repeated awards under quota failure, rejected cross-topic mastery and recovery after writes become available.

## Why this test exists

A successful final picture or correct structured rule alone must not bypass independent transfer. Stored awards must remain scoped and idempotent even during persistence failures. These cases combine the actual shipped curriculum with the shared state machine rather than checking only isolated sample definitions.

2026-11-12: all 13 cases passed; full 330/330. Per-case steps, expected/actual and durations: [joint case register](../FOUR-MODULE-TEST-CASES.md). Full report: [TEST-RESULTS.md](../TEST-RESULTS.md).
